About 6G

6G is the fastest internet ever and the base of the digital future.

We combine fiber and wireless to deliver low latency and high speeds — in dense cities and in the Alps. Our architecture uses multi‑path routing and edge nodes to keep experiences snappy and resilient.

Our network is the most reliable across Switzerland: smart routing, redundancy and active monitoring 24/7. We test at altitude and in basements so that your calls and downloads simply work.

Coverage

From Geneva to St. Gallen, from Lugano to Basel — SwissStar keeps you connected where it matters. We expand coverage in rural areas while densifying cities.

We continuously add capacity and optimize spectrum usage so you’re online at home, in the office and on the move — even during festivals and peak travel seasons.
