About us

We started with a simple idea: make internet in Switzerland faster, more reliable and more accessible.

Over the years we built a network trusted by thousands nationwide. Companies trust us — like Swisscom and Sunrise do — because quality and service come first. Our culture combines engineering excellence with customer empathy.

From backbone design to last‑mile experience, we measure, test and tune relentlessly to keep latency low and stability high — not just in labs, but where you live and work.

CEO: Viacheslav Zakharov. Headquarters: Uster. From here we operate our infrastructure and support across all cantons.

Our values

Innovation — we ship improvements weekly: smarter routing, better last‑mile tuning, automated failover and proactive monitoring. We adopt tech when it raises reliability and user happiness — not just because it’s trendy.

Responsibility — transparent pricing and plain language. We design for privacy and security by default, perform regular audits, and fix root causes instead of masking symptoms.

Team — people build networks. We mentor, document, run blameless post‑mortems and celebrate learning. We invest in apprenticeships and professional growth across DE/FR/IT regions.

Sustainability — fewer truck rolls, smarter power usage at the edge, and hardware reuse programs reduce our footprint. We measure energy per gigabit and optimize for real‑world efficiency.

Customer obsession — we track time‑to‑solution, not time‑to‑close. Every ticket improves documentation and tooling so the next user gets help faster.

Craft — clean architecture, reproducible deployments, and living playbooks. Quality is a habit visible in uptime, not a promise in ads.
